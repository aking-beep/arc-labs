# Product brief template

Written when a record is decided `incubate` and moves to `incubator/building/`. This is
the first product-shaped document an idea gets — before it, everything is an experiment.

Copy `BRIEF.md` to `incubator/building/<id>-brief.md` and fill it in. The brief is
reviewed at the quarterly graduation review and is a prerequisite for
[Gate 5](../../docs/GRADUATION_CRITERIA.md#gate-5--incubate--graduate).

A brief that takes more than two pages is describing a portfolio, not a product.
