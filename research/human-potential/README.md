# Human potential

How human capability is built, measured, and sustained. The reading that sits behind the `human-performance` domain, and the oldest body of knowledge Labs draws on — most of what matters here was established before software was involved.

## Current questions

- Which motivation findings survive translation into software, and which are artefacts of a lab
- What is actually measurable about capability outside a controlled setting
- Skill acquisition: where structured progression beats volume, and where it does not
- What measurement does to the person being measured
