# ideas/ — `stage: idea`

Written down, not started. The cheapest place in Labs to be wrong.

**Entry:** anyone opens a record with `labs new`. The five questions must be answered —
an idea that cannot answer them is a wish, and wishes do not get directories.

**Exit:** [Gate 1](../../docs/GRADUATION_CRITERIA.md#gate-1--idea--experiment) — an owner,
a timebox, a `review_by` date, and at least one behavioural or economic evidence signal
with a target. Then `labs move <id> --to experiment`.

**No timebox here.** An idea is allowed to sit. What it is not allowed to do is sit
forever: past 90 days `labs validate` warns, and the monthly portfolio review either
promotes it or kills it. Ideas do not improve by waiting.

Most records should die in this directory. That is the directory working.
