# validating/ — `stage: experiment` | `prototype` | `validating`

Work in flight. Everything here is timeboxed, owned, and carries a written kill condition.

| Stage | Means | Typical timebox |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `experiment` | Smallest buildable thing, in flight | 1–7 days |
| `prototype` | Works end-to-end for one real person | 1–3 weeks |
| `validating` | In front of real people, collecting the named evidence | 2–6 weeks |

Three stages share one directory because they share one property: **a decision is due on
a date.** That is what this directory is for — holding the things that owe an answer.

**Exits:** Gates [2](../../docs/GRADUATION_CRITERIA.md#gate-2--experiment--prototype),
[3](../../docs/GRADUATION_CRITERIA.md#gate-3--prototype--validating), and
[4](../../docs/GRADUATION_CRITERIA.md#gate-4--validating--incubate). Or `kill`, at any
point, which is the most common and most useful exit.

**Weekly review covers this directory specifically.** Fifteen minutes, every record,
every overdue date decided in the room. See
[EXPERIMENTATION.md](../../docs/EXPERIMENTATION.md#weekly-review-script).

Iterating is fine — up to three times. A fourth means the hypothesis is not testable at
this scale, and the validator says so.
